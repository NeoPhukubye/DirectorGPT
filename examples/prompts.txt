# Example Film Prompts for DirectorGPT

## Drama
"A retired musician hears a song that reminds them of a lost love, leading them on a journey through their memories."

## Horror
"A night security guard at a museum notices the exhibits are slightly different each morning, and tonight he catches them moving."

## Sci-Fi
"In a world where dreams can be recorded, a technician discovers someone else's dream that predicts the future."

## Comedy
"A professional mime accidentally gets hired as a corporate consultant and must navigate meetings without speaking."

## Romance
"Two rival food truck owners on the same street slowly realize their recipes taste better when combined."

## Thriller
"A translator at the UN realizes a diplomatic message contains a hidden assassination plot."
